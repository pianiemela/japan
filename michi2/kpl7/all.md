3. Here is the correction and breakdown for your sentence:

**手袋（てぶくろ）を使（つか）います。** *(I use gloves.)*

---

### Key Corrections

* **Particle Placement:** The particle **を** (object marker) belongs directly after the item being used, not after 手 (手**に** → **手を** / **手袋を**).
* **Word Choice:** To ask "what do you use *on your hands*", Japanese uses the location particle **に** with the body part: **手（て）に** (on your hands) **何（なに）を** (what) **使（つか）いますか** (do you use?).

---

### Revised Question & Standard Answer

* **Question:** 寒（さむ）いとき、手（て）に何（なに）を使（つか）いますか。
*(When it's cold, what do you use on your hands?)*
* **Answer:** **手袋（てぶくろ）を使（つか）います。**
*(I use gloves.)*
