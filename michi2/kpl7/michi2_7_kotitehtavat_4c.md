
ほんをよむところはなんといいですか
---

### Question Revision

* **Corrected:** 本（ほん）を**読（よ）む**場所（ばしょ）は何（なん）**と言（い）いますか**。
*(What do you call a place where you read books?)*

*Note: You can use **ところ** (place) or **場所（ばしょ）** (place/location).*

---

### Answer Options

The answer depends on whether you mean a public library, a bookstore, or a reading room/study area:

* **図書館（としょかん）** (*toshokan*): Library.
* **本屋（ほんや）** (*hon'ya*) / **書店（しょてん）** (*shoten*): Bookstore.
* **読書室（どくしょしつ）** (*dokusho-shitsu*): Reading room.

**Complete Answer Sentence:**

> **図書館（としょかん）と言（い）います。**
> *(It is called a library.)*

1. A natural beginner-level version would be:

**デパートで洋服などを着てみるところは日本語で何と言いますか？**
*Depāto de yōfuku nado o kite miru tokoro wa Nihongo de nan to iimasu ka?*

However, if you mean **“What do you call the place in a department store where you try on clothes?”**, the most natural wording is:

**デパートで洋服などを試着するところは日本語で何と言いますか？**
*Depāto de yōfuku nado o shichaku suru tokoro wa Nihongo de nan to iimasu ka?*

→ **What do you call the place in a department store where you try on clothes?**

The word you are probably looking for is **試着室（しちゃくしつ）** — **fitting room / changing room**.


2. 


“Where do Finnish people go naked?”**

A natural beginner-level version is:

**フィンランド人が裸で入るところはどこですか？**
*Finrando-jin ga hadaka de hairu tokoro wa doko desu ka?*

→ **Where do Finnish people go in naked?**

**Answer:**
**サウナです。**
*Sauna desu.*
→ **It’s a sauna.**

Here, **裸（はだか）** = naked, and **裸で入る** = enter/go in naked.


3. A natural corrected version is:

**家で料理をするところはどこですか？**
*Ie de ryōri o suru tokoro wa doko desu ka?*

→ **Where in the house do you cook?**

If the intended answer is **“kitchen”**, then:

**キッチンです。**
*Kicchin desu.*
→ **The kitchen.**

A slightly more natural version is:

**家で料理をする場所はどこですか？**
*Ie de ryōri o suru basho wa doko desu ka?*

Here **場所（ばしょ）** = place/location, while **ところ** is more beginner-friendly.

Yes. If you want an answer other than **キッチン**, depending on the intended vocabulary, you could use:

* **台所（だいどころ）** — kitchen; a traditional/common Japanese word
* **リビング** — living room
* **ダイニング** — dining room
* **食堂（しょくどう）** — dining room / dining hall

If the question is intended to have **台所** as the answer, I would phrase it:

**家で料理をするところはどこですか？**
*Ie de ryōri o suru tokoro wa doko desu ka?*
→ **Where do you cook at home?**

**台所です。**
*Daidokoro desu.*
→ **The kitchen.**

For a beginner Japanese exercise, **台所** is a good alternative to **キッチン**.
